## Overlap vs Differentiation

### Where Existing Tools Overlap with Our Concept

- Many platforms record change orders, T&M tickets, and billing.
- Some offer change-order workflows, approvals, and basic reporting.
- Job-costing tools track cost vs budget.

### Where Our MVP Differs

| Dimension | Typical Construction Software | Construction Revenue Audit |
|-----------|-------------------------------|----------------------------|
| Primary job | Manage the work & documents | Independently verify that money reconciles |
| Input model | Live system of record | Import existing exports (CSV/XLSX/PDF) |
| Output focus | Status, schedules, field data | Financial exceptions & evidence-linked findings |
| Calculation transparency | Often opaque or buried | Explicit calculation + source records on every finding |
| Setup friction | High (replace or deeply integrate) | Low (upload what you already have) |
| Independence | N/A (it is the system of record) | Deliberately independent verification layer |

## Positioning Statement (Internal)

> Your construction software records what happened.  
> We check whether the money actually reconciles.

## Pricing Observation

Public pricing for enterprise construction platforms is often quote-based and higher than what a 10–100 person specialty contractor may comfortably pay for a secondary audit tool.  
This creates room for an affordable, focused product — but pricing must be validated with customers, not assumed.

## Competitive Discipline

- Do not expand MVP scope merely because a competitor has a feature.
- Do not remove scope merely because a competitor exists.
- Compete by being excellent at the narrow promise: finding missed, under-billed, or poorly supported revenue.
